Railroad Tycoon Remake - Game Data Folder
=========================================

To play the game, you need the original DOS Railroad Tycoon files.
Please place the following original game files directly into this 'data' folder:

Required files:
- *.PIC (e.g. EASTUS.PIC, WESTUS.PIC, BRITAIN.PIC, EUROPE.PIC,
         SPRITES.PIC, TRACKS.PIC, STATION.PIC, LOCOS.PIC, FACES.PIC,
         LOGO.PIC, PAGE0.PIC through PAGE9.PIC, TITLE.PIC, etc.)
- *.PAN (e.g. TITLEM.PAN, WOOD2.PAN, IRONM.PAN, FLOODM.PAN,
         ENGFLDM.PAN, WRECKM.PAN)
- *.DTA (e.g. CITIES0.DTA, CITIES1.DTA, CITIES2.DTA, CITIES3.DTA)

(Note: FONTS.RR, SPRITES_extra.png, and TRACKS_extra.png are already included).

Once the original files are in this folder, launch the game!
